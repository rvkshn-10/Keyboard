Keyboard61 enclosure prototype - 2026-10-12

Files and units
keyboard61-enclosure.step is the assembly for Fusion, in millimetres.
base.step and switch-plate.step are the actual manufactured/printed solids.
PCB, knob and encoder solids inside the assembly are reference envelopes.
Do not print the assembly or the case-preview.stl.
For a paid service: print base.stl x1, switch-plate.stl x1, plate-spacer.stl x4.
For a 256mm printer: print base-left/right and switch-plate-left/right x1 each,
join-strap.stl x2, plate-spacer.stl x4. Join base halves with four M3 bolts and nuts.
Support the underside uniformly when using protruding joining straps.

Mounting
Case outside: 333 x 107 x 27.6mm excluding keys and knob.
Keyboard PCB remains 295 x 100 x 1.6mm, with its underside at z21mm.
Four bosses match the actual PCB mounting holes. Pilot holes are 2.7mm for M3.
Use four 3.5mm plate spacers between PCB top and plate underside.
The 1.5mm switch plate has 61 openings and ten stabilizer clearance slots.
Switch-plate top is 5mm above PCB top. Check switch latch engagement on a sample.
Plate attaches through the existing four PCB mounting holes into the printed bosses.
Trial M3 x12mm screws and verify engagement before final assembly.

Volume encoder
Selected accessory: Adafruit product 377 Rotary Encoder + Extras.
Vendor https://www.adafruit.com/product/377
Includes encoder, knob and mounting hardware. Panel hole is 7.2mm diameter.
Mount in the right side panel, x315 y91mm, using the supplied washer and nut.
Underside walls limit rotation of the encoder body; verify the delivered body fits.
A -> Pico physical pad25 (GP19), B -> pad26 (GP20).
S1 -> pad27 (GP21), C and S2 -> GND, for example Pico pad28.
Wire directly to the free Pico socket solder pads. Encoder is not on the keyboard PCB.
Use five insulated conductors. Prevent shorts at exposed joints and secure strain relief.
KMK uses pull-ups. Do not connect encoder contacts to 5V or VBUS.
Both keymap layers: turn left volume down, turn right volume up, press mute.
If physical direction is reversed, set is_inverted=True in encoder_handler.pins.
Reboot after installing boot.py so USB consumer-control reports become available.

USB access
An underside access opening exposes the area around the inward-facing USB plug.
The floor slot continues to a rear cable exit. No USB extension is required.
Actual plug insertion, bend radius, BOOTSEL and reset access need a fit check.

Checks and remaining fit work
CAD solids pass OpenCascade validity checks. See model-checks.json.
Electrical DRC/ERC and schematic parity are checked separately.
Physical hardware has not been assembled or tested.
Nominal Pico header-pin clearance at Tab/Q is only 0.255mm using a 14mm square
switch body and 0.7mm pin diameter. This excludes actual housing shape and tolerances.
Do not treat this as verified manufacturing clearance. Trial fit or move the socket
before ordering if the actual switches or protruding solder joints intrude.
Confirm stabilizer cutouts, switch fit and cable access before purchasing a full print.
Paid-service pricing is a separate quote, not the $6-7 filament-only estimate.
