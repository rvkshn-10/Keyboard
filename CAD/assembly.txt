Keyboard61 slim prototype — 2026-10-13
Current assembly: keyboard61-enclosure.step or keyboard61-enclosure.f3d.
Case: 356 x 112 x 17.1mm. Case height reduced 38%; keycap reference top is 31.5mm.
Print base.stl x1, switch-plate.stl x1, plate-spacer-pack.stl x1; trim sprues.
Old split-print files are archived; the revised 356mm case needs a large printer
or paid service. The previous $19.61 resin quote does not apply to this revision.

PCB bottom at 10.5mm; plate bottom at 15.6mm; floor 2mm; walls 2.5mm.
Use existing Pico H headers soldered directly to the keyboard PCB; tall female
sockets are not used in this slim version. Trim protruding tails flush after
soldering. Actual Tab/Q switch housing and solder clearance remain unverified.
The KiCad Pico model has about 1.96mm nominal clearance above the case floor.
No exact WS stabilizer CAD was located. Verify switch, stabilizer, keycap,
controller, encoder, plug, screw engagement and print tolerances before ordering.

Portrait display window: 44.8 x 59.2mm, bottom margin 17.2mm, knob gap 13.6mm.
Selected candidate: ShillehTek AA317 2.8-inch ILI9341 SPI, $10.99 plus shipping/tax.
The TFT body is a generated 50x86mm dimensional envelope, not manufacturer CAD.
Support geometry is provisional: verify delivered mounting hole locations and
the header/SD-card protrusions, then revise bosses to match. Do not force-fit.
Wiring and optional display firmware: ../firmware/tft-setup.txt and tft_setup.py.
The optional case-wired display is not yet included in the main schematic.

MX switch and ALPS encoder downloads are compatibility substitutes, not exact
Gateron or Adafruit part models. Keycaps are generic envelopes with recessed
legends; they do not have functional stem sockets and are not print-ready.
Purchased keycaps remain standard vendor parts, not custom engraved products.
Actual hardware has not been assembled or tested. Root PCB fabrication geometry
is unchanged. Archive folder contains the older enclosure for reference only.
