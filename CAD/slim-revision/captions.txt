1. Inspect a downloaded switch model
01-downloaded-switch-model.png
Downloaded and inspected the Acheron MX-compatible model. This is a substitute for the selected Gateron switch, not its exact manufacturer CAD.

2. Reduce the case height
02-thin-shell.png
Generated a shell with a 2mm floor and lower walls, reducing case height from 27.6mm to 17.1mm. The wider right strip reserves space for a portrait screen.

3. Add PCB mounting bosses
03-mounting-bosses.png
Added the four mounting bosses at the PCB hole coordinates. PCB underside is now 10.5mm above the origin. The slim version requires directly soldered Pico headers instead of tall sockets.

4. Cut controller access openings
04-usb-access.png
Added the underside USB pocket, cable channel, BOOTSEL and reset openings to the thinner base. Actual cable insertion still needs a fit check.

5. Cut the key and stabilizer openings
05-switch-openings.png
Generated 61 switch openings and stabilizer clearance slots in the 1.5mm plate.

6. Add the upper-right knob mount
06-encoder-mount.png
Added the encoder mounting hole and underside support walls. Exact Adafruit encoder fit remains unverified.

7. Reserve the tall portrait display
07-oled-window.png
Replaced the tiny OLED idea with a 44.8 by 59.2mm window below the knob. The bottom margin is 17.2mm and the gap to the knob is 13.6mm. The TFT body is a dimensional envelope.

8. Place the downloaded switch models
08-switches-installed.png
Placed 61 instances at the PCB switch centers. These generic MX models help inspect the assembly; exact housing clearances are still unverified.

9. Engrave the keycap legends
09-keycaps-installed.png
Generated recessed legends 0.35mm deep in the generic keycap CAD. These are reference keycaps without functional MX mounting sockets; purchased keycaps are not physically modified.

10. Assemble the revised keyboard
10-complete-slim-assembly.png
Combined the slim case, KiCad PCB, downloaded switch and encoder substitute models, engraved keycap references, spacers and portrait TFT envelope in Fusion.
