Design references and limits (2026-10-15)

Display: ShillehTek AA317 2.8-inch ILI9341 SPI TFT. The model uses the manufacturer mechanical drawing: 50 x 86 x 1.6 mm board, 44 x 76 mm mounting-hole pitch, 43.2 x 57.6 mm active area, 14-pin main header and separate 4-pin microSD header. The model is dimension-based; delivered hardware has not been measured or test-fitted.
Vendor page: https://shillehtek.com/products/2-8-spi-tft-lcd-touch-screen-module-240x320-ili9341-for-arduino
Mechanical drawing: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0837/4340/8415/files/MSP2807_mechanical_dimensions.pdf?v=1784420081

Schematic: main TFT SPI uses Pico GP22 (DC), GP26 (SCK), GP27 (MOSI), GP28 (CS). The display's RESET and backlight LED are tied to 3V3; touch pins are left unused. The separate microSD CS is held high and its other pins are unused. Display backlight current is specified by the vendor as 30-100 mA. KiCad ERC: 0 violations, 0 errors, 0 warnings. Display operation has not been tested on hardware.

Keycaps: 61 generic caps have 0.35 mm recessed legends and nominal Cherry MX cross sockets (4.1 mm cross, 1.17 mm arm, 3.8 mm socket depth) with 0.10 mm per-side CAD clearance. Ten stabilizer positions receive cross sockets. Print the three-clearance gauge first and test the actual switches/stabilizers; fit is not physically verified.
MX geometry reference: https://github.com/nicola-sorace/custom-keycap-generator/blob/main/stem.py

Assembly: the case is 356 x 112 x 17.1 mm. CAD estimates 2.12 mm between the TFT header tips and case floor and 1.96 mm Pico-model clearance. Those margins are small and depend on real module orientation, trimmed pins, print tolerances, wiring, and the selected switch/stabilizer models. Q/Tab clearance, key travel, cable access and display mounting remain physical fit checks.
